Local Windows x64 crash-capture prototype

The capture DLL is built from Crashpad, with its mini_chromium and zlib
dependencies, plus the reviewable OneKey bounded-memory C ABI wrapper.
The accompanying license texts retain the upstream notices, including
mini_chromium's ICU-derived code and Crashpad's getopt compatibility code.
